# literature glossary

**Vahana** — Sanskrit for a deity's divine vehicle or mount; each god and
goddess is traditionally carried by one.

**Hansa** — Sanskrit for swan; the vahana of Devi Maa Saraswati, the
goddess of knowledge and the written word.

**Etymology** — the study of where a word comes from, and how its meaning
has traveled to reach us.

---

### on this site's name

**The witty swan** takes its name from Devi Maa Saraswati, whose vahana is
the hansa. **Dhawal**, the name behind this site, means *pure white* in
Sanskrit — fitting, for a swan.
