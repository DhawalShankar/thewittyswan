### [School records](https://capecomorinschool.com), made simple
Built for a school that was drowning in old paper records. Scanned pages
of handwritten student data now get read and organized automatically,
locked behind proper access controls so only the right staff can reach
the right records.

A platform where people practice languages with each other and turn that

### [AuralAce](https://auralace.vercel.app)
A small tool that cleans up and reshapes audio — stretching, smoothing,
adding depth — and lets you see the difference in the sound before and
after.
