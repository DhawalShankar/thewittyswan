# welcome to the witty swan

Come in, sit a while.

Here you will find two kinds of thoughts living side by side — one that
studies how networks breathe and how to keep them safe, and one that
simply loves a good book and the quiet it brings. They do not compete for
space here. Some days call for firewalls, other days call for footnotes.

Wander through whenever you like. Stay for the technical, return for the
literary, or the other way around — either way, there will be something
here waiting for you next time.
